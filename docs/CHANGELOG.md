## 2026-10-05
- WTH-168: the weather of a past moment. `HistoricalProvider.getAt(lat, lon, at)` over Open-Meteo's archive returns the same `WeatherData` the timeline takes (it is anchored on `current.time`, so nothing in the timeline changed) plus `reconstruction`: a reanalysis, the model by date (IFS 9 km from 2017, ERA5 25 km before), a confidence by age (high, medium, low), and what the model has no value for (visibility, UV, air quality). Moments under 10 days old are refused; no invented rain chance, no zero for a missing value. 21 tests on a real recorded answer (Milan, 11 August 2003: 36.6 C, a clear dry noon), through the timeline, the look and the fingerprint. From 2017 the data is the IFS, which Open-Meteo assembles from the operational model's own runs: the record calls it a forecast archive, not a reanalysis. Every quantity the model leaves empty is listed, and an hour with no temperature or weather code is dropped, never read as zero or as a clear sky. Two faults of the archive found on real answers are corrected: its days are cut at the offset of today for every date (the days and their extremes are taken from the hours, by the clock the place kept), and its `is_day` was 0 for all of 28 August 1963 in Washington (day and night come from the sun's times). 32 tests on four recorded real answers (Milan 2003, Berlin 1989, Washington 1963, Mumbai 2003); a place with an odd offset (India, Nepal) has its hours on the half or quarter hour in UTC, so the nearest instant is taken; where the archive's offset of today is half a day from the one then, its own days are kept and marked partial. `docs/RECORDS_SAMPLES.md`: the five historical facts for the validation page (Berlin 1989, Tokyo 1964, Washington 1963, London 1969, Paarl 1990), each with the weather reconstructed beside what is documented: two agree, two partly, one not verified. Nothing on the page uses it yet. Commercial use needs Open-Meteo's paid plan.
