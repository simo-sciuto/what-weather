Verified 2026-10-04 and one defect fixed: `toDailyFromForecast` took a day as whole when it had 8 three-hour steps, but the day the clocks go forward has 23 hours and 7 steps: 34 zone-days of 2026 (America/Denver on 8 March, Atlantic/Halifax, Africa/Cairo on 24 April, ...) were flagged `partial`, dropped from the week and, for today, sent `tempRange` to the 24-hour fallback. A day is now whole when its steps start in its first three hours, end in its last three and have none missing in between, by elapsed time at the place (`localDayBounds`, new in `formatters.ts`, right for 23, 24, 25 and half-hour days and for a midnight that occurs twice, as in the Azores): no interior day of a year is partial in any of 418 zones (tested). Not a defect, documented: a three-hourly day's min and max are the extremes of its steps, as the hourly curve shows them, so they can fall short of the true extremes by a degree or so (the code chose that over the forecast's own temp_min/temp_max, which run degrees wide). Hourly providers give the day's own min and max.
