Rivers and lakes: touch, highlight and contextual information from reliable sources. Weather stays tied to the active weather point and updates when the movement ends; no continuous requests while scrolling and no invented weather along routes. Mountains and valleys: zoom reveals the land progressively (contours, relief, peaks, heights, lakes, streams) with studied graphic hierarchies; elements selectable when identifiable; no dashboard of labels and cards. Later, not in the first version: an optional "Segui il corso" mode (the camera travels a selected river gradually, interruptible at any time), which needs complete and reliable geometries and a check of the data sources. Trails are a possible later extension, distinct from rivers; no routing and no hiking navigation yet. The map must be interesting even when the user searches for nothing and touches nothing. Failed weather update: the state is designed in the artifact; the position shown (crosshair, candidate) and the data actually loaded stay distinct, and the previous data and colours stay on screen marked as not updated.
